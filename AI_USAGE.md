## AI Governance & Verification Policy Statement

1. **Human-in-the-Loop Ownership:** All AI-generated outputs (datasets, schemas, code, and documentation) were systematically reviewed, manually verified, and executed against automated test suites by the responsible lead before merging into the main branch.
2. **Deterministic Ground-Truth Enforcement:** No AI pipeline was permitted to self-validate or grade its own generated output. All compliance scoring is computed deterministically via Pipeline 2 Python code.
3. **Zero-Plagiarism & Originality Assurance:** All policies, test scenarios, and adversarial cases were synthesized specifically for the fictional entity *Apex Global Solutions Inc.* to ensure 100% clean IP compliance.
